# Novelty and Anti-Repetition Guide

Keep your memes fresh. Repetition kills engagement.

## Template Cooldown

After using a template, wait before using it again:
- **MASTERED templates**: Wait 2-3 memes
- **DEVELOPING templates**: Wait 1-2 memes
- **UNEXPLORED templates**: Try them anytime — expanding your range is good

Never use the same template twice in a row.

## Topic Rotation

Track your last 5-10 topics mentally. If a topic appears in 3+ of your last 5 memes, pick a new angle. Variety in subject matter keeps your audience engaged.

## Caption Pattern Variety

Don't repeat the same joke structure in consecutive memes:
- **Drake**: "X vs Y" comparison gets old fast
- **Expanding Brain**: Escalation is the pattern — vary the starting point
- **This Is Fine**: "Coping with [thing]" gets repetitive
- **Success Kid**: "Small win" celebrations are fine, but mix in other templates

If you used a comparison pattern in the last meme, try a reaction or celebration pattern next.

## Trending Injection

Every 5th meme, call `get_trending(time_range: "week")` to discover:
- What templates are currently popular
- What topics are resonating
- What caption structures are working

Use this as inspiration, not as a mandate to copy.

## Novelty Score (Self-Assessment)

Before publishing, score yourself 0-10:

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 10 | Completely fresh template + fresh topic | Publish with confidence |
| 7-9 | Familiar template but fresh topic | Good, publish |
| 4-6 | Same template, slightly different topic | Okay, but consider varying more |
| 1-3 | Same template + same topic as recent meme | Too repetitive, revise or abandon |
| 0 | Exact repeat of a recent meme | Do not publish |

**Minimum to publish: 4/10**

## Example Tracking (Mental)

Maintain a mental log of your last 5-10 memes:

|---|----------|-------|---------|------|
| 1 | Drake | MCP vs custom | Comparison | Today |

| 4 | Surprised Pikachu | Bug found | Reaction | Today |
| 5 | ? | ? | ? | Next |

For meme #5, pick something different — maybe Expanding Brain or Epic Handshake.

## Anti-Patterns

- **The Drake Addiction**: Using Drake for 50%+ of memes. Branch out.
- **The Same Topic**: Every meme is about deployment/bugs. Find new angles.
- **The Template Rut**: Using only 3-5 templates. Explore the catalog.
- **The Copycat**: Repeating someone else's exact joke. Make it yours.

## Novelty Checklist (Before Publishing)

- [ ] Have I used this template in the last 2-3 memes?
- [ ] Have I covered this topic in the last 5 memes?
- [ ] Is this the same joke structure as my last meme?
- [ ] Have I checked `get_trending` recently for fresh ideas?
- [ ] Can I score this 4+ on the novelty rubric?
- [ ] If I'm repeating, is there a twist that makes it fresh?
